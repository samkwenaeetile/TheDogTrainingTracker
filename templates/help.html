Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find guidance and answers to common questions about using Dog Training Tracker.

How the Tracker Works

1. Create an Account

Register for an account and log in to access the dog training features.

2. Add Your Dog

Create a dog profile with information such as the dog's name, breed and age.

3. Record Training

Add training sessions and record the activity, duration, notes and progress.

Account Help

How do I create an account?

Select Register from the navigation menu and enter your username, email address and password.

How do I log in?

Select Login and enter the email address and password associated with your account.

Why can't I access the dashboard?

The dashboard and training features are protected. You must be logged in before accessing them.

Dog Profiles

How do I add a dog?

Log in, select Add Dog and complete the dog profile form.

Can I change my dog's information?

Yes. Open View Dogs and select Edit for the dog you want to update.

Can I delete a dog profile?

Yes. Dog profiles can be removed from the View Dogs page.

Training Sessions

How do I record training?

Select Training Sessions, choose your dog and enter the details of the training activity.

Where can I see previous training?

Training History displays the sessions that have been recorded for your dogs.

Can I change or delete a training session?

Yes. Training History provides options for editing and deleting existing training records.

Still Need Help?

Something isn't working

Check that all required information has been entered correctly and try the action again.

If you cannot access a protected page, make sure you are logged in to your account.
