Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Dog Training Tracker.

Account Help

How do I create an account?

Select Register from the navigation menu and enter your username, email address and password.

How do I log in?

Select Login and enter the email address and password associated with your account.

Why can't I access the dashboard?

The dashboard and training-management features are protected. You must be logged in before accessing them.

Dog Profiles

How do I add a dog?

Log in, select Add Dog and complete the dog profile form.

Can I change my dog's information?

Yes. Open View Dogs and select the Edit option for the dog you want to update.

Can I delete a dog profile?

Yes. Dog profiles can be removed from the View Dogs page.

Training Sessions

How do I record training?

Select Training Sessions, choose your dog and enter the details of the training activity.

Where can I see previous training?

Training History displays the sessions that have been recorded for your dogs.

Can I change or delete a training session?

Yes. Training History provides options for editing and deleting existing training records.

Still Need Help?

If you experience a problem, check that all required information has been entered correctly and try the action again.
